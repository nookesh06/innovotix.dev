A PRACTICAL LEARNING JOURNEY

From first spark
to working prototype.

Learning areas below are a suggested structure for schools. Innovotix's published website confirms robotics, IoT, AI and electronics learning, but does not publish a fixed class-wise syllabus.

01 / DISCOVER

Classes 1–3

Explore movement, shapes, mechanisms, simple circuits and cause-and-effect through guided activities.

  • Build-and-play challenges
  • Simple mechanisms
  • LED and switch basics
02 / CREATE

Classes 4–5

Introduce sensors, motors and beginner logic while building small interactive models.

  • Sensor-based activities
  • Motor control concepts
  • Block-based coding ideas
03 / ENGINEER

Classes 6–8

Combine coding, electronics and mechanical design to make robots that respond to their environment.

  • Robotics and automation
  • Microcontroller concepts
  • IoT starter projects
04 / INNOVATE

Classes 9–10

Explore connected systems and structured prototyping with testing, documentation and presentations.

  • IoT and AI foundations
  • Prototype development
  • Project showcase
