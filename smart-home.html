SMART HOME SOLUTIONS

Connected comfort,
room by room.

These are common smart-home solution categories shown for exploration. Actual brands, device compatibility and service availability should be confirmed with Innovotix before purchase or installation.

01 / LIGHTING

Smart lighting

Dimmable lights, colour temperature control, lighting zones and preset scenes for daily routines.

  • Warm, neutral and cool tones
  • Room-wise brightness control
  • Scheduled lighting routines
02 / CONTROL

Smart switches

Connected switchboards and control interfaces that can bring selected circuits into one system.

  • App and wall-panel control
  • Switch scheduling
  • Centralised device control
03 / SENSING

Sensors & safety

Presence, motion and environmental sensing can trigger selected automations when supported.

  • Motion-triggered lighting
  • Door and window sensing
  • Alerts and routines
04 / AUTOMATION

Scenes & routines

Coordinate multiple compatible devices around a single activity, schedule or user action.

  • Welcome home
  • Movie and relax scenes
  • Good night / away mode
A DAY WITH SMART LIGHTING

One home.
Different moments.

Example lighting routines inspired by common smart-home design patterns.

06:30 / MORNING

Wake gently

Warm lights gradually brighten to help the room transition into the day.

18:30 / EVENING

Welcome home

Entry and living areas illuminate as a welcome scene, with comfortable warm tones.

20:30 / ENTERTAINMENT

Movie time

Main lights dim while selected accent lighting remains on for a relaxed viewing setup.

22:30 / NIGHT

Quiet mode

Most lights switch off while a soft night light can remain active where needed.
