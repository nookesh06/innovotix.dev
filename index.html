ABOUT INNOVOTIX

Build curiosity.
Enable possibility.

Innovotix is an education technology initiative based in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, focused on bringing robotics and STEM learning into schools.

01

Our mission

To create accessible, interactive STEM environments where students can experiment with robotics, IoT, AI and electronics — and develop creativity, confidence and problem-solving through making.

The company describes a focus on interactive robotics labs in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, including support for school lab setup and practical learning.
